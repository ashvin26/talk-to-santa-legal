Privacy Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Talk to Santa is an app for parents and guardians. You set up profiles for your children, and they can have a short live call with an AI Santa. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who helps us run it, and how you can delete it. "We" and "us" means [OPERATOR NAME], who runs Talk to Santa.

The short version

  • The app is used by parents. Children do not have accounts, and a parent sets up every call.
  • Santa is an AI character, not a real person.
  • We don't record calls. We keep no audio, no video and no transcript of what is said, and Santa has no memory from one call to the next.
  • Santa can't see your child. The camera stays off for the whole call. Santa only hears them.
  • Santa only knows what you add to your children's profiles. You can edit or delete a profile, or your whole account, in the app at any time.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Children's privacy and parental consent

Talk to Santa is meant to be used by a parent or guardian, with their child. Because children talk on the calls, and because the profiles describe children, we treat this as information about children and follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) principles of notice, parental consent, minimal collection and parental control.

  • Only an adult can create an account. Children never sign in or create accounts.
  • Consent before the first call. Before any call, the app shows "How Santa works" (what Santa is, that calls aren't recorded, that the camera stays off, and which services run the call). You confirm that you are the child's parent or guardian and agree to these terms. We record the date and the version of that wording you agreed to. If the wording changes, we ask you again.
  • A Kid Mode PIN keeps the parent screens (profiles, purchases, account settings) behind a code that stays on your phone.
  • You stay in control. You can review, change or delete your children's information at any time in the app, delete your whole account, or ask us to do it for you (see "Your choices"). You can also simply stop using the app, and no more information will be collected.

If you believe a child has given us information without a parent's consent, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it.

What we collect

About you (the parent)

  • Email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code and to contact you about your account. If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives us an email address, which may be a private relay address.
  • Your first name, if you give it, so the app can greet you.
  • Your consent record: when you agreed and to which version of "How Santa works".
  • Video call credits and purchases: how many video calls you have, and a record of each purchase or refund (an event ID, the product and the number of calls). Apple handles payment; we never see your card or bank details.

About your children (entered by you)

You decide what to add. A profile needs only a first name and an age; everything else is optional. A call can also include no profile at all, in which case Santa simply asks who he is talking to.

  • First name (up to 30 characters) and, optionally, how to pronounce it
  • Age (2 to 12)
  • Interests and hobbies, favorite school subject, favorite color and favorite animal
  • Pet type and pet name
  • Best friend's first name
  • A recent good deed Santa can mention
  • Call language (English or Spanish)

Please use first names only and don't add anything you wouldn't want Santa to say out loud. You can have up to five child profiles.

About calls

  • Call details: when a call started and ended, how long it lasted, whether it was a phone call or a video call, which of your child profiles were on it, and, for video calls, the time slot you booked and whether the call happened.
  • Your feedback, if you give it after a call: a star rating, quick tags (such as "Clear sound") and an optional comment.
  • Your child's voice, live only. During a call, your child's voice is sent in real time to the services listed below so Santa can understand and answer. It is processed as the call happens and is not recorded or stored by us. No camera video is captured.

On your phone only

The app keeps your sign-in session, your Kid Mode PIN, your language choice and a copy of your consent version on your phone. The PIN never leaves the device.

What we don't collect

No call recordings, transcripts or call summaries; no memory of past calls; no camera video; no photos, contacts or location; no advertising identifiers; and no third-party analytics or advertising tools.

How we use it

  • To run calls: Santa uses the profile information you chose for the children on that call, plus the call's language and length, to greet them by name and talk about things they like.
  • To run your account: signing you in, booking video calls, keeping track of free phone calls and video call credits, and restoring purchases.
  • To keep calls safe and working: content safety rules for Santa, fixing problems, and preventing abuse.
  • To improve the app, using the feedback you choose to give.

We do not use your children's information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent anyone's information.

How long we keep it

  • Account, child profiles, call details and feedback: until you delete them or delete your account. A deleted child profile is removed straight away.
  • Call audio and video: not kept by us. It exists only while the call is live.
  • Purchase records (event ID, account ID, product and number of calls): kept after an account is deleted, because we need them for accounting and refunds. They contain no information about your children. Apple and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records.
  • Service logs: our providers keep short-lived technical logs (for example times, IDs and error messages) to run and secure their services.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete a child's profile at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your children's profiles, your call history, your bookings and your feedback straight away. It can't be undone. Unused video call credits are lost when the account is deleted.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your or your child's information, or to stop collecting it, by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL]. We may need to confirm you are the account holder first.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Economic Area), you may have further rights, such as to access, correct, delete or move your information, or to complain to a data protection authority. Email us and we will help.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Your data is protected by access rules so that each parent can reach only their own account and children. Video call rooms are private and can be joined only by the phone that started the call. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects it.

Where information is processed

We and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If a change affects what Santa knows or how calls work, we will show "How Santa works" again and ask for your agreement before the next call.
